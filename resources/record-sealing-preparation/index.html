Record sealing preparation guide

Preparing for a Nevada record-sealing request

General information to help organize case records before requesting Nevada record-sealing document preparation.

⚖

General information only. This guide is not legal advice and eligibility or requirements may vary.

Build a complete case list

Collect every known case number, arresting agency, court, approximate arrest date, charge, disposition, and completion date. Missing cases can delay the preparation process.

Collect available records

Useful materials may include criminal-history reports, court dockets, discharge documents, proof that fines or obligations were completed, and identification records.

Confirm that the information is current

Record-sealing requirements and eligibility depend on the facts of each case and current law. A licensed Nevada attorney can advise you about eligibility or legal strategy.

Prepare for agency and court steps

Record sealing may involve multiple agencies and administrative steps. LawMart can prepare selected documents from your instructions and explain non-legal filing steps.

Protect your privacy: Do not send Social Security numbers, full financial account numbers, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email or a website form.
