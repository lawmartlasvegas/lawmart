Information you provide

When you call, email, or use the consultation form, you may provide your name, contact details, selected service, and a general description of the documents you want prepared.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, understand requested services, communicate about document preparation, and operate or improve the website.

Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to collect general device, browser, referral, campaign, and usage information so LawMart can understand website performance and advertising results.

Do not submit sensitive information

Do not submit Social Security numbers, full account numbers, passwords, medical records, or similarly sensitive information through the public website form or ordinary email.
