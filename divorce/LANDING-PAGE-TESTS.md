# LawMart Divorce Landing Page Variants

All variants keep the remainder of the Phase 3.16 divorce page intact and change only the first two sections.

- `/divorce/affordable/` — “An uncontested divorce doesn’t have to cost thousands.”
- `/divorce/flat-fee/` — “Uncontested divorce paperwork for a flat $500.”
- `/divorce/simple/` — “Agree on the divorce? We’ll help with the paperwork.”
- `/divorce/no-kids-no-property/` — “No kids. No house. Divorce paperwork for $500.”
- `/divorce/long-separated/` — “Separated for years? It may be time to finish the paperwork.”

The $500 language refers to LawMart's document-preparation fee. Court and third-party costs remain separately disclosed on the page. The long-separated version deliberately avoids promising that a default judgment or publication is available in a particular matter; those steps depend on facts and court rules.
