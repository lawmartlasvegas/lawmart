What happens after filing?

Will your spouse sign or participate?

Agreed divorce

Your spouse signs or participates

Continue through the normal court-processing steps for the documents you approved.

No signature

Your spouse does not sign

  1. Arrange formal service. A separate process-service fee applies.
  2. Allow the response period. The response period is generally 21 days after service.
  3. Prepare default paperwork if appropriate. If no response is filed, LawMart can prepare client-directed default documents for submission.

Location unknown

You do not know where your spouse is

  1. Document reasonable search efforts. Attempts to locate and serve your spouse must be recorded.
  2. Request court-authorized publication. If permitted, publication and related third-party fees apply.
  3. Complete the required waiting steps. After publication and the applicable response period, LawMart can prepare client-directed default documents if appropriate.

Preparation times depend on receiving complete client information. Court, service, publication, and response timelines vary. Default and publication procedures require court approval and do not occur automatically.

Questions about this service

Important answers before you start.

LawMart provides document preparation—not legal advice or representation.

Can LawMart tell me what I should ask for in my divorce?

No. LawMart does not give legal advice or recommend legal positions. You decide the terms and provide the instructions used to prepare the documents.

Does LawMart handle contested divorces?

LawMart prepares documents at the client’s direction. Matters involving disputes, unclear rights, or contested hearings may require advice from a licensed attorney.

Can you prepare documents when children are involved?

Document preparation may include parenting-plan, custody, and support-related forms when the client provides the required decisions and information.

How long does preparation take?

Timing depends on the complexity of the requested documents and how quickly complete information is provided. The expected timeline is discussed before work begins.
