Pricing questions

Before you get started.

Are filing fees included?

No. Court, agency, USCIS, recording, service, notary, translation, and other third-party charges are separate.

Who receives government filing fees?

Government fees are generally paid to the applicable court or agency. We will identify administrative payment instructions, but you remain responsible for confirming current amounts.

Can immigration packages cost more than the listed amount?

Yes. Multi-form or unusually document-intensive packages may require a higher preparation fee. You will receive the preparation price before work begins.

Does the fee include legal advice?

No. LawMart is a document preparation company and does not provide legal advice, eligibility analysis, strategy, or representation.

Can I pay in installments?

Ask us about available payment arrangements before work begins. Any arrangement should be confirmed in writing.
