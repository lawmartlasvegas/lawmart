Common questions

Know what to expect.

Clear answers about LawMart’s role and how our document-preparation service works.

Is LawMart a law firm?

No. LawMart is a document preparation company and does not provide legal advice or representation.

Are filing fees included in the $500 fee?

No. Court filing, government, recording, process-server, and other third-party charges are separate from LawMart’s preparation fee.

Can I talk to someone before paying?

Yes. Phone consultations are free. For in-person consultations, choose an available time first, then pay the $50 reservation deposit to confirm the appointment. The deposit is credited toward your service if you proceed.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes, by appointment and subject to availability. Submit the intake form or call us to arrange a time.
